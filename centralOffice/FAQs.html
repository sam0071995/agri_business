Aswer of Frequently Ask Questions


1. What is dealer e-wallet?

The Dealer E wallet is a process under which dealers can do smoothly transactions of HSRP receipt generation through Virtual Balance of money deposited with FTA HSRP Solutions. Pvt. Ltd. And can be used as payment option at the time of HSRP Receipt generation.back to top


2. How does e-wallet works?

It’s a Simple Procedure of Just 3 Steps. Deposit Money In Bank Either through Ch/DD or NEFT/RTGS Login to www.hsrpgujarat.com and generate Deposit Update Request Soon the Virtual Balance will be appeared in Your Log In ID and Unlimited Receipts of HSRP can be generated.back to top


3. Why would I use e-wallet?

Payment of HSRP can be done without Net banking/ Credit Card/ Debit Card No need to visit RTO for Payment of HSRP or receipt generation Dealer E wallet is 24 X 7 Available service provided by FTA HSRP Solutions Pvt. Ltd. It is time saving and simple procedure of payment and receipt generation Dependency on a specific bank is reduced, as when any of the provided banks go offline, you can still generate receipts from your e-Wallet account. Dealers can directly be in touch with FTA Personnel for any query or in case of any support regarding HSRP back to top


4. Who can enroll for dealer e-Wallet?

Any authorized dealers who are registered and deals with vehicles can enroll for dealer e-Wallet.back to top


5. How can I enroll for dealer e-wallet account?

Sign up with us @ www.hsrpgujarat.com and registered yourself. An E Mail Confirmation will be provided after registration with Unique Username, Log IN Password and Transaction Password ( combination of alfa numeric with special character, min length 6 character)back to top


6. How can I recharge my e-wallet account?

Recharge can be done by NEFT/RTGS or by Cheque/DD. For detailed description on recharge please refer How to Deposit money/make payments in docket.back to top


7. How to take new rights for HSRP?

New rights can be taken by contacting us @ dealer.ewallet@hsrpgjarat.com back to top


8. How to reset password?

Click on forget password provided on the website and you will receive registered password on registered email ID, later by login you can reset the password from change password form. back to top


9. What is way out in case of password forgotten or lost?

In both the case you can receive the registered password on registered email ID by clicking on forget password button provided on the website. back to top


10. Is there a minimum balance for maintaining e-Wallet?

Yes, minimum balance of Rs.4000 is to be maintained for security reason and smooth transactions. back to top


11. Can I use e-wallet for transaction on other websites?

No, Dealers e-wallet is made for only HSRP receipt generation process. So the money of e-wallet will not be used at other websites for transaction. back to top


12. How to check whether payment acknowledgment converted into advance receipt?

For checking the same you have to refer the reports section. Click on the print pending advance receipt report and get the desired results to print. back to top

15. Whom to contact in case of any Banking Related query?

For any banking related queries mail us at dealer.ewallet@hsrpgujarat.com . we will dully vet your queries and resolved within provided timeline. back to top


16. What is Basic System requirement for Dealer e wallet?

Basic hardware Windows XP, Windows 7 back to top


17. How e wallet is secured?

Two passwords login & Transaction are used in e-wallet payment which makes it more secure. back to top


18. Can bulk HSRP receipt generation possible? How?

Yes, you can pay for multiple receipts by adding more than one record at a time and process the payment. back to top


19. How to re print HSRP receipt?

You can re-print the receipt from the Reprint report which is provided in the main menu in reports tab. back to top


20. How to track DUR?

We have provided a DUR report. You can track it by entering the DUR number or by selecting the date of DUR filled. back to top

22. How can I know the available balance?

Available balance will be shown at every screen after login. On the receipt generation page on the top most part it shows the available balance. back to top
